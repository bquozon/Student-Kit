Privacy

No accounts, no tracking. You never log in and we set no analytics or advertising cookies. Visits are counted anonymously as daily totals, with no IP address stored: a visitor is recognised for one day through a hash whose key is thrown away at midnight.

“I use this” stores a random id in a cookie on your browser, so you can't vote twice and can undo your vote. We keep only a hash of that id next to the services you marked, nothing that tells us who you are.

Suggestions keep only what you type into the form. We don't store who sent them.

Cookies: the vote id (2 years), your last chosen city (1 year) a security token for forms, and for the site's owner a login and a “don't count my visits” flag. That's all.

Datenschutz (kurz)

Kein Konto, kein Tracking. Besuche zählen wir nur anonym als Tagessummen, ohne IP-Adressen. Für „I use this“ speichert dein Browser eine zufällige ID, damit du nur einmal abstimmen kannst; wir behalten nur einen Hash davon. Bei Vorschlägen speichern wir nur, was du ins Formular schreibst.
