Privacy

Browsing needs no account. We set no tracking or analytics cookies. We count visits anonymously: daily totals only, no IP address stored. A visitor is recognised for one day through a hash whose key is thrown away at midnight.

If you log in, we store your university email, your university (from the email) and the services you mark with “I use this”. Your suggestions and reports are linked to your account so moderators can check them and so we can email you the result. Nobody else sees your email.

Cookies: a login cookie (90 days), your last chosen city, and a security token for forms. That's all.

Delete your data: email the site owner from your university address and we delete your account and your marks.

Datenschutz (kurz)

Zum Stöbern brauchst du kein Konto, und wir setzen keine Tracking-Cookies. Besuche zählen wir nur anonym als Tagessummen, ohne IP-Adressen. Wenn du dich anmeldest, speichern wir nur deine Uni-E-Mail, deine Universität und die Dienste, die du mit „I use this“ markierst. Auf Anfrage löschen wir dein Konto.
